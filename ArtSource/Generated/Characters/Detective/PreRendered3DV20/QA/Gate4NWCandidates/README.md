- `walk_nw_04_authority_candidate_1024.png` is its normalized 1024×1024 RGB authority form.
- `walk_nw_07_authority_candidate.png` is the accepted raw built-in output for the corrected phase-07 authority.
- `walk_nw_07_authority_candidate_1024.png` is its normalized 1024×1024 RGB authority form.
- `walk_nw_07_authority_candidate_v2_1024.png` is a rejected oversized experiment retained for audit.
